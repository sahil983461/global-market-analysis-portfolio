1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE & PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of this project is to develop an end-to-end Executive Portfolio Analytics platform that enables investment firms and portfolio managers to monitor and analyze diversified investment portfolios across Stocks and Cryptocurrencies.

The organization manages multiple investors with different risk profiles and investment preferences. Senior management requires a centralized Business Intelligence solution to evaluate portfolio performance, identify high-performing assets, monitor investment trends, analyze investor behavior, and assess portfolio risk for strategic investment decisions.

The dashboard provides actionable insights by consolidating portfolio transactions, market performance, investor information, and asset classifications into a single interactive Power BI reporting platform.

The project answers critical business questions across six analytical areas:

Page 1: Executive Portfolio Overview & Investment Summary

Page 2: Stock Market Performance & Sector Analysis

Page 3: Cryptocurrency Performance & Portfolio Allocation

Page 4: Portfolio Performance, Asset Allocation & Investment Distribution

Page 5: Investor Behavior, Investment Trends & Risk Segmentation

Page 6: Portfolio Risk Profile & Investor Risk Analysis


2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs) & BUSINESS LOGIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To evaluate portfolio performance and investment activity, the dashboard implements the following analytical KPIs using DAX.

* Total Portfolio Value

Formula =
SUM(Quantity × Purchase Price)

Business Purpose:
Measures the total monetary value invested across all assets.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Total Investment

Formula =
Sum of all investment transactions

Business Purpose:
Tracks total invested capital across the portfolio.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Total Investors

Formula =
Distinct Count of InvestorID

Business Purpose:
Measures active investor participation.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Investment per Investor

Formula =
Total Portfolio Value ÷ Total Investors

Business Purpose:
Evaluates average investor contribution.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Total Transactions

Formula =
Count of Investment Transactions

Business Purpose:
Measures overall trading activity.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Brokerage

Formula =
Average(Brokerage)

Business Purpose:
Measures average transaction cost incurred during trading.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Asset Allocation %

Formula =
Investment Value by Asset Type ÷ Total Portfolio Value

Business Purpose:
Analyzes capital allocation between Stocks and Cryptocurrencies.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Buy vs Sell Investment

Formula =
Investment Value grouped by Buy/Sell Transaction Type

Business Purpose:
Monitors investment behavior and market participation.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Investment by Risk Profile

Formula =
Investment Value grouped by Investor Risk Profile

Business Purpose:
Evaluates capital distribution across Conservative, Moderate, and Aggressive investors.


3. DATA DICTIONARY (CRITICAL METRICS EXPLAINED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The database consists of multiple transactional and dimensional tables supporting portfolio analytics.

AssetID
Unique identifier assigned to each financial asset.

AssetName
Name of the investment instrument (Stock or Cryptocurrency).

AssetType
Classifies the asset as Stock or Cryptocurrency.

Sector
Business sector associated with the stock asset.

InvestorID
Unique identifier assigned to each investor.

InvestorName
Registered investor name.

RiskProfile
Investor risk classification:
• Conservative
• Moderate
• Aggressive

TransactionID
Unique identifier for every investment transaction.

BuySell
Indicates whether the transaction represents a Buy or Sell operation.

Quantity
Number of units traded.

Price
Transaction execution price per unit.

Brokerage
Brokerage fee charged for the transaction.

Open
Opening market price.

High
Highest trading price.

Low
Lowest trading price.

Close
Closing market price.

Volume
Total traded volume for the asset.

Date
Transaction or market trading date.
